Agendex Calendar Planner
Diary, year planner & to-dos

Agendex brings your appointments, birthdays and to-dos together in a paper-style diary on iPhone, including iPhone Duo, iPad and Mac. A full, searchable user guide is built into the app: on iPhone and iPad open Settings › Help › Agendex Help; on Mac choose Help › Agendex Help (Command-?).

Getting started

  1. Choose where your planner is stored in Settings › Calendar Storage: Local Storage keeps everything on this device; Apple Calendar uses your existing calendars and reminders.
  2. Add an event with + in Agenda, birthdays in Anniversaries, and tasks in To-dos.
  3. If you use Local Storage, make a backup from time to time in Settings › Back Up & Export.

Common questions

  • I can't see my calendars or reminders. Choose Apple Calendar in Settings › Calendar Storage and allow full access. If you declined before, turn access back on in your device's Privacy & Security settings under Calendars and Reminders.
  • My events disappeared after switching storage. Each storage keeps its own data. Switch back to see them, or move them with a complete backup and Import or Restore.
  • Some events aren't showing. Check the Filter button in Agenda; a number on it means some calendars or tags are hidden.
  • I can't edit an event. It's in a read-only calendar, such as a subscription or an invitation from someone else. Duplicate it into one of your own calendars.
  • Week view or the year grid is missing. They need a wide window: iPad in landscape, iPhone Duo opened in landscape, or Mac.
  • Alerts don't appear. With Local Storage, allow notifications for Agendex in your device's settings.
  • Changes from another device haven't arrived. Choose Refresh Now in Settings. Your calendar service decides when changes are delivered.

Backup, export and import

Agendex backs up complete calendars to a standard iCalendar (.ics) file and imports .ics files from other apps. Local Storage data stays on the device, so back it up before deleting Agendex or changing devices.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad: iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. iPhone Duo adaptation requires iOS 27.1 or later.
  • Mac: macOS 26 or later.
  • Apple Calendar storage: full access to Calendars, and to Reminders for to-dos.

Contact and privacy

Email appsupport@app.sdmail.net for help. Include your device model, operating-system version, the app version and build, which storage you use, and what happened. Please don't send private calendar details unless they're needed to explain the issue.

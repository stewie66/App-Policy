Zero Hour

Zero Hour: Up Next
Event & Milestone Tracker

Count down to birthdays, trips, anniversaries and personal milestones on iPhone and iPad. Add a Home Screen widget or check your next event on a paired Apple Watch.

Create an event

Tap +, enter a title, choose a date and time, then pick an icon and colour. Tap Save. Zero Hour shows the time remaining in days, hours, minutes and seconds.

View and edit events

Tap an event in All events to show its countdown. Swipe left or right on the large countdown card to browse upcoming events. Tap the card to edit it, or open the event's … actions menu and choose Edit event…. Use that menu to delete an event.

Set an alarm or notification

In the event editor, enable Alarm at zero, Notification at zero, or both. Choose a future date and time, then save and allow the requested permission.

Alarms can sound through Silent Mode and Focus. Notifications follow your device's notification settings. Both are scheduled by the system and can work while Zero Hour is closed.

If an alert cannot be saved, read the message in the app and check Zero Hour's permissions in Settings. Notification sounds and visibility also depend on your notification and Focus settings. To stop an event's future alerts, turn off its alert switches and save, or delete the event.

Add a widget

Touch and hold your Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, search for Zero Hour, and choose a small or medium Next event widget. It displays your earliest upcoming event. Tap it to open that event in the app.

Use Apple Watch

Install and open the Zero Hour companion on an Apple Watch paired with your iPhone. Add and edit events on the iPhone; the next countdown appears on the Watch as the devices exchange data. Keep the devices paired and connected. iPad does not pair directly with Apple Watch.

Where are events stored?

Events are stored locally. Zero Hour has no account or cloud synchronisation service, so separate iPhones and iPads have their own event lists. Widgets use the events saved on the same device. Event information is shared with your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.
  • For the Watch companion: watchOS 26 or later and a paired iPhone.
  • System permission is required for each type of alert you enable.

Contact

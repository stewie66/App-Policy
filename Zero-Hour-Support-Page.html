Zero Hour: Up Next
Event & Milestone Tracker

Count down to birthdays, trips, shopping events and personal milestones on iPhone, including iPhone Duo, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro. Add an iPhone or iPad Home Screen widget or check your next event on a paired Apple Watch.

Create an event

Choose Add event (+), enter a title, choose a date and time, then pick an icon and colour. Choose Save. Zero Hour shows the time remaining in days, hours, minutes and seconds. Shopping is one of the ten available icons.

View, edit or delete an event

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, select an event in All events to show its countdown. Swipe horizontally on the large countdown card to browse events, select the card to edit, or open the event's … actions menu and choose Edit event…. Use that menu to delete the event.

On Apple Vision Pro, select an event in the sidebar. Use the toolbar to edit, delete or Open countdown window. Separate countdown windows can be placed in your space. Search and the sidebar filter help you find events.

Deleting an event also deletes it from your other devices when they sync using the same Apple Account.

Sync events with iCloud

Events sync automatically between iPhone, iPad, the native Mac app and Apple Vision Pro through your private iCloud database. There is no separate Zero Hour account or manual sync button.

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device.
  2. Enable iCloud access for Zero Hour in the device's settings, and make sure iCloud has available storage.
  3. Open Zero Hour on each device with an internet connection. Existing local events join that account's synced list when syncing becomes available.

Event titles, dates and times, icons and colours sync. Alarm and notification choices stay separate on each device. Offline edits are saved locally and sync when iCloud and a connection are available. Changes may take time to appear; background delivery depends on the system.

If the same event is edited offline on multiple devices, the app resolves competing edits using their modification dates; a deletion takes precedence over a conflicting edit. Sync is not a version-history or backup feature. To avoid competing edits, let devices sync before editing the same event again.

If events are not syncing

  • Check that each device uses the same Apple Account and that iCloud is enabled for Zero Hour.
  • Check the internet connection and available iCloud storage.
  • Open Zero Hour on each device and allow time for changes to arrive. There is no need to enable an event notification to sync.
  • Install the latest app version on every device. Older versions may not understand new event icons such as Shopping.
  • If a problem continues, contact support before resetting or removing app data, especially if a device has changes that have not synced.

Widgets update from the local events on their own device after the host app processes changes. The Watch follows its paired iPhone; neither is an independent source of iCloud updates.

Set an alarm or notification

In the event editor, find Alerts on this device. On iPhone and iPad, choose Alarm at zero, Notification at zero, or both. On Mac and Apple Vision Pro, choose Notification at zero. Choose a future time, save, and allow the requested permission.

On iPhone and iPad, alarms can sound through Silent Mode and Focus. Notifications follow the device's notification and Focus settings. Alerts are scheduled by the operating system and can work while Zero Hour is closed.

Enable alerts separately on each device where you want them. After a synced date change, alerts already enabled on that device are updated when the app processes the change. To stop an alert, turn off its switch and save, or delete the event. If an alert cannot be saved, read the app's message and check system permissions.

Add a Home Screen widget

On iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, search for Zero Hour, and choose a small or medium Next event widget. It displays your earliest upcoming event. Select it to open that event in the app.

Use Apple Watch

Install and open the Zero Hour companion on an Apple Watch paired with your iPhone. Create and edit events in the main app. Events reach the Watch through its paired iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity service; the companion displays the next countdown and does not connect directly to iCloud. Keep the devices paired and connected. iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro do not pair directly with this Watch companion.

Use iPhone Duo or Mac

On iPhone Duo, the layout adapts between the outer display, the opened inner display and partially folded positions. Your selected event and an in-progress edit stay with you when folding or unfolding.

On Mac, install the native Mac app. The countdown and event list appear side by side when the window has enough room. Use Command-N to create an event.

Manage or remove your data

You can use Zero Hour locally without iCloud. To stop future syncing, disable iCloud access for Zero Hour in the device's settings. Doing so does not delete data already in iCloud. Signing out, switching accounts or uninstalling also does not erase existing iCloud data. Signing out can leave an event cache on the device; caches for different Apple Accounts are kept separate.

To remove a synced event, delete it in Zero Hour while using the relevant Apple Account, then let your devices sync. Offline copies update when they next connect. A deletion marker remains without the event content to stop an old offline copy from restoring the event. Where available, Apple's iCloud storage settings let you manage the app's stored iCloud data. Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad: iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Full iPhone Duo adaptation requires iOS 27.1 or later.
  • Native Mac app: macOS 26 or later.
  • Native Apple Vision Pro app: visionOS 26 or later.
  • Watch companion: watchOS 26 or later and a paired iPhone.
  • Syncing: the same Apple Account, iCloud access for Zero Hour, available iCloud storage and an internet connection.
  • Alerts: system permission for each type enabled on each device.

Contact and privacy

Email appsupport@app.sdmail.net for help. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version/build and a description of what happened. If the issue concerns syncing, say which devices are affected and whether they use a prerelease or development version. Do not send your Apple Account password or private event information unless the latter is needed to explain the issue.
